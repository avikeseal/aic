SKETCHES

FALLEN ANGEL

BETTER TO REIGN IN HELL THAN SERVE IN HEAVEN - Pencil on paper, inspired by Alexandre Cabanel's painting which was then inspired by John Milton's epic poem Paradise Lost located at Musee Fabre in Montpellier, France.

CURSE OF THE BLOOD MOON

EMOTIONS LEFT RIPPED AT THE SEAMS THE GAME IS OVER OR SO IT SEEMS - Pencil on paper inspired by Children of Bodom's tenth and final studio album - Hexed highlighting the exhaustation and frustation when dealing with someone who is full of empty promises, fake emotions, and exhausting drama.

CRIES IN THE NIGHT

BUT IM LIVIN TO LOSE AND DYIN TO WIN WITH THESE PEOPLE AROUND HERE MY PATIENTCE WEARS THIN - Pencil on paper, inspired by the track of the same name by W.A.S.P from their 1985 album The Last Command. The track deals with inner turmoil and loss of idenity that often comes with trying to maintain a public image.
